# Video Sources and Content Categories

This document describes the 10 panoramic videos used in Interruption360.

The original video stimuli are not redistributed as part of the Interruption360 behavioral dataset unless permitted by the corresponding source datasets. Users should obtain and use the videos according to the licenses and terms of the original public datasets.

The 10 videos used in Interruption360 come from two public 360-degree video datasets:

1. **Xu et al., CVPR 2018**  
   Yanyu Xu, Yanbing Dong, Junru Wu, Zhengzhong Sun, Zhiru Shi, Jingyi Yu, and Shenghua Gao.  
   *Gaze Prediction in Dynamic 360° Immersive Videos.*  
   In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2018, pp. 5333–5342.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2018.00559

2. **Xu et al., TPAMI 2019**  
   Mai Xu, Yuhang Song, Jianyi Wang, Minglang Qiao, Liangyu Huo, and Zulin Wang.  
   *Predicting Head Movement in Panoramic Video: A Deep Reinforcement Learning Approach.*  
   IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 41(11):2693–2708, 2019.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2018.2858783

---

## Category Definition

For Interruption360, the 10 videos are grouped into the following content categories:

- **Scenic:** static landscape or low-motion panoramic scenery.
- **Dialogue:** user conversation or human-centered conversational scenes.
- **Fighting:** combat or fighting scenes with strong motion and action.
- **Singing/Dancing:** music, singing, dancing, or performance-centered scenes.
- **Game/Action:** intense game or action scenes with high visual dynamics.

These categories are used to summarize the content diversity of Interruption360 and are not intended as fine-grained semantic labels.
